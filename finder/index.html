How It Works

Drink or Menu?

After setting up your bar, choose your path. One Perfect Drink walks you through three quick questions and lands on a single recommendation. Show Me a Menu asks the same three questions but hands you a curated list of options to browse and pick from yourself.

Vibe, Spirit, Occasion

Each question lets you pick up to three answers — tap to select, tap again to deselect. Picking more than one broadens your results (e.g. Gin or Rum). "Surprise me" and "Any point" skip the question entirely.

Search

Two ways to look things up. By Name — know what you want? Type any part of a drink name to go straight to the recipe. By Ingredient — got a pint of blueberries to use up? Type an ingredient to see every drink that calls for it, with the ones you can make right now sorted to the top. Tick Only show drinks I can make to hide the rest.

My Bar

Tell us what's behind your bar and we'll prioritize drinks you can actually make. Choose a preset tier (Essentials, Advanced, Deep Cuts), check off ingredients manually, or add your own custom items in the right category. Your bar saves automatically. Use the search box at the top to jump straight to an ingredient.

Makeable Icons

A green check means you have everything a drink needs. An amber ~ means you're missing something, but it has an easy swap already in your bar (like lemon for lime). Drinks are ranked with the ones you can make first.

No This?

See a No this? button next to an ingredient? Tap it for a substitution suggestion if you're missing something.

PDF This Menu

From the Menu view, tap PDF This Menu to lay out whatever's on screen as a clean, printable drink list — then save it as a PDF or print it from the share sheet.

Share

Found something good? Tap Share This Recipe on any result to send it to someone.
